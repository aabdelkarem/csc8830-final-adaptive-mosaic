Adaptive Mosaic Art

A photograph becomes a field of color tiles. Detailed regions receive smaller tiles, while smooth areas keep larger blocks.

How the image guides the artwork

Each tile uses its average RGB color. The algorithm considers splitting a tile into four quadrants and chooses the split that most reduces squared color error. It repeats until the tile budget is reached. Ink borders and the limited palette are artistic choices applied afterward.

What the measurement means

The displayed error compares the original crop with unrounded tile means before palette and border effects. Lower error means closer color reconstruction; it does not establish that people prefer the artwork. Both methods use the same crop and tile count. The Python report evaluates three included photographs, without a user study.
